# Purpose

This document defines how the Studio manages opportunities, experiments, solutions, and products.

The Portfolio represents the value creation output of the Studio.

---

# Portfolio Principle

Not every idea becomes a product.

The Studio manages uncertainty through experimentation and validation.

Only validated opportunities progress into sustainable products.

---

# Portfolio Categories

## Opportunities

Potential areas where meaningful value may exist.

Examples:

- Emerging problems
- Market changes
- Technology shifts

---

## Research Initiatives

Structured investigations into potential opportunities.

Purpose:

Understand the problem and possible solutions.

---

## Experiments

Small-scale tests designed to validate assumptions.

Purpose:

Reduce uncertainty.

---

## Solutions

Validated approaches that demonstrate potential value.

---

## Products

Solutions transformed into sustainable offerings.

---

# Portfolio Decision Rules

## Continue

Evidence indicates meaningful value.

Move forward.

---

## Iterate

Potential exists but more learning is required.

Continue experimentation.

---

## Stop

Assumption is not validated.

Preserve knowledge and close initiative.

# Evolution Principle

The Portfolio evolves continuously.

New initiatives are created from validated opportunities.

Existing initiatives may grow, transform, or end based on evidence.
