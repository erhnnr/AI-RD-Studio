# AI Research & Development Studio Architecture

Version: 0.1  
Status: Draft  
Last Updated: 2026-08-05

---

# Purpose

This document defines the structural architecture of the AI Research & Development Studio.

It describes how the Studio is organized as an AI-native research and development system.

This document defines structure only.

Operating processes and individual project details are managed separately.

---

# Architecture Overview

The Studio consists of five primary layers:
AI Research & Development Studio

    |
    |
    ├── Foundation
    |
    ├── Operating System
    |
    ├── AI Workforce
    |
    ├── Knowledge
    |
    └── Portfolio

---

# 1. Foundation

Purpose:

Defines the identity, vision, mission, and fundamental principles of the Studio.

Contains:

- Vision
- Mission
- Core Principles
- Long-term direction

Foundation changes rarely.

---

# 2. Operating System

Purpose:

Defines how work flows through the Studio.

Responsible for transforming signals into validated value.

Main capabilities:

- Strategy
- Research
- Engineering
- Validation
- Product

---

# 3. AI Workforce

Purpose:

Defines the AI-supported capabilities that operate inside the Studio.

Examples:

- Strategy Worker
- Research Worker
- Engineering Worker
- Validation Worker
- Product Worker

The Workforce evolves according to real needs.

---

# 4. Knowledge

Purpose:

Stores accumulated learning produced by the Studio.

Contains:

- Research findings
- Decisions
- Experiments
- Lessons learned
- Validation results

Knowledge is the long-term memory of the Studio.

---

# 5. Portfolio

Purpose:

Contains products, experiments, and initiatives created by the Studio.

Projects may start, evolve, or end.

The Studio architecture remains independent from individual projects.

---

# Evolution Principle

The Studio evolves through validated learning.

New capabilities are added only when real requirements emerge.

Complexity is introduced only when it creates measurable value.
