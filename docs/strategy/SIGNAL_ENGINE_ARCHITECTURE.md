# Purpose

The Signal Engine is the discovery layer of the Studio.

Its purpose is to continuously observe the external world, identify meaningful changes, detect potential problems, and create research opportunities.

The Signal Engine does not create products directly.

It creates validated opportunities for investigation.

---

# Core Principle

The Studio does not start with ideas.

The Studio starts with signals.

A signal is an observable change that may indicate:

- A new problem
- A market opportunity
- A technology shift
- A user need
- A social or economic change

---

# Signal Sources

Potential signal sources:

## Market Signals

Examples:

- Industry changes
- Customer complaints
- Emerging needs
- Business model changes

---

## Technology Signals

Examples:

- New AI capabilities
- Scientific breakthroughs
- New infrastructure

---

## Human Signals

Examples:

- Repeated user problems
- Workflow inefficiencies
- Unmet needs

---

## Data Signals

Examples:

- Statistical changes
- Trends
- Anomalies

# Signal Evaluation

Each signal should be evaluated by:

## Impact

Does this represent a meaningful problem or opportunity?

## Urgency

Is the need increasing?

## Feasibility

Can a solution realistically be created?

## Strategic Fit

Does it align with Studio capabilities?

---

# Opportunity Conversion

A signal becomes an opportunity when:

- A real problem is identified
- Evidence exists
- Potential value is measurable
- Further research is justified
