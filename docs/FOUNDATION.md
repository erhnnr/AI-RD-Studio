# AI Research & Development Studio Foundation

Version: 0.1  
Status: Draft  
Last Updated: 2026-08-05

---

# Purpose

AI Research & Development Studio exists to systematically discover valuable real-world problems, develop solutions, validate outcomes, and transform successful solutions into sustainable products.

The Studio is not built around technology creation alone.

The Studio is built around creating measurable real-world value.

---

# Identity

The Studio is an AI-native research and development organization.

AI is not only a tool used by the Studio.

AI is a fundamental capability layer that supports research, analysis, engineering, validation, and product development.

---

# Core Principles

## Problem First

Real-world problems are the starting point.

Technology selection comes after understanding the problem.

---

## Core First

Strong foundations are created before complexity is introduced.

Every system evolves from a minimal working core.

---

## Evidence First

Important decisions require evidence.

Assumptions must be researched, tested, and validated.

---

## Validation Driven

Ideas do not become products without validation.

The Studio prioritizes proven value over speculation.

---

## Knowledge Preservation

Every decision, experiment, success, and failure becomes part of the Studio's long-term memory.

Knowledge compounds over time.

---

# Evolution Principle

The Studio evolves through validated learning.

New capabilities are added only when real needs emerge.

Complexity is introduced only when it creates measurable value.
