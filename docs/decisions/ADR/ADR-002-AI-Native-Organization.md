# ADR-002: AI-Native Organization

Date: 2026-08-06

Status: Accepted

---

# Context

Traditional organizations use technology as a supporting tool.

The Studio is designed differently.

Artificial intelligence is not only a tool used by the organization.

AI is a fundamental capability layer integrated into research, analysis, engineering, validation, and product development processes.

---

# Decision

The Studio adopts an AI-Native Organization model.

AI capabilities will be integrated into core operating processes.

The Studio will develop systems where:

- AI supports decision making
- AI accelerates research
- AI assists engineering
- AI improves validation
- AI preserves and extends organizational knowledge

---

# Principle

The Studio operates with a human-AI collaboration model.
Human Intelligence

    +

Artificial Intelligence

    ↓

Enhanced Organizational Capability


AI does not replace the purpose of the organization.

AI increases the ability of the organization to discover, learn, and create value.

---

# Consequences

Positive:

- Faster research cycles
- Increased ability to analyze complex domains
- Continuous knowledge accumulation
- Scalable problem-solving capability

Negative:

- Requires careful system design
- Requires validation of AI outputs
- Requires clear responsibility boundaries

---

# Future Impact

All future Studio systems should consider AI as a core organizational capability.

AI Workforce, Knowledge Systems, and Operating Processes should evolve around this principle.
