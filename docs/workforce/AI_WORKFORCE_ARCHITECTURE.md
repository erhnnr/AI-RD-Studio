# Purpose

This document defines the AI-supported workforce structure of the Studio.

The AI Workforce provides specialized capabilities that support research, engineering, validation, and product development.

The workforce is not limited to autonomous agents.

It may include:

- AI workers
- Human operators
- Human-AI collaboration systems

---

# Workforce Principle

Each worker exists to provide a specific capability.

A worker must have:

- Clear responsibility
- Defined input
- Defined output
- Measurable contribution

# Core Workers

## Strategy Worker

Purpose:

Detect meaningful signals and identify potential opportunities.

Input:

- External information
- Market changes
- Technology trends
- Problems

Output:

Strategic Brief

---

## Research Worker

Purpose:

Investigate opportunities and validate assumptions.

Input:

Strategic Brief

Output:

Research Report

---

## Engineering Worker

Purpose:

Transform validated knowledge into working systems.

Input:

Research Report

Output:

Working Solution

---

## Validation Worker

Purpose:

Determine whether solutions create real-world value.

Input:

Working Solution

Output:

Validation Result

---

## Product Worker

Purpose:

Transform validated solutions into sustainable products.

Input:

Validation Result

Output:

Product Artifact + Feedback

---

# Knowledge Relationship

All workers contribute to the Studio Knowledge layer.


Worker Output

  ↓

Knowledge

  ↓

Future Decisions


---

# Evolution Principle

New workers are created only when existing capabilities cannot satisfy a validated need.

The Studio avoids unnecessary complexity.
